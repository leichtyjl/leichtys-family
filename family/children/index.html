John and Salome’s family

Their Four Children.

Simon, Iona, Jacob, and Carl each belong in the immediate family story. Three of them became the founders of today’s descendant branches.

Simon Leichty 1915–1992 Simon Branch Iona Leichty 1917–1920 Remembering Iona Jacob Leichty 1920–2012 Jacob Branch Carl Leichty 1925–2007 Carl Branch

An important distinction

Four children does not mean four branches.

Iona is remembered here with her siblings. She died in childhood and did not create a descendant branch. The reunion’s three descendant branches come through Simon, Jacob, and Carl.
