Visiting Goshen

Planning your trip.

A few practical notes to help family coming in from out of town.

Lodging

Everyone is welcome to make their own lodging arrangements for the weekend of our family reunion in Goshen, Indiana, on Saturday, June 12, 2027. Here are a few options to consider:

  • Hampton Inn GoshenComfortable hotel with breakfast, indoor pool, and fitness center.
  • Holiday Inn Express & Suites GoshenFamily-friendly option with breakfast, indoor pool, and suites.
  • Best Western Inn GoshenConvenient and more budget-friendly option with breakfast.
  • Inn on South 5thCharming historic bed & breakfast in downtown Goshen.
  • Spring View Bed & BreakfastPeaceful country setting with a cozy B&B atmosphere.
  • Rose Lane Farm Bed & BreakfastBeautiful rural setting surrounded by Amish and Mennonite farmland.

You can also check Airbnb for vacation rentals or use Google to search for additional hotels and lodging options in the Goshen area. Please choose whichever option works best for your family — these are suggestions only, with no reserved room blocks or special rates.

Airports

South Bend International Airport (SBN) — The closest airport with regular commercial flights, approximately 30–40 miles from Goshen. This is probably the easiest airport for most family members flying in.

Other options to check include:

  • Fort Wayne International (FWA)
  • Kalamazoo/Battle Creek (AZO)
  • Grand Rapids (GRR)
  • Chicago Midway (MDW)
  • Chicago O’Hare (ORD)

Check flight prices and schedules for SBN first, but it may be worth comparing the other airports for better fares or nonstop flights.

Restaurant recommendations

There are lots of great places to eat in and around Goshen! Here are some recommendations for the reunion weekend:

Goshen favorites

  • VenturiAward-winning, authentic Neapolitan pizza in downtown Goshen. A great choice for dinner.
  • The Table at 108Italian-inspired food in a comfortable, family-friendly setting.
  • Goshen Brewing CompanyCasual atmosphere with American-style food.
  • Hoppers Pike Street GrillCasual American dining with generous portions.
  • Biebs & AshBurgers, sandwiches and fries in downtown Goshen.
  • Universal TamalGreat choice for Mexican food and homemade tamales.
  • Maple Indian CuisineA flavorful option for Indian food.

Breakfast & casual dining

  • The Tipsy BiscuitPopular spot for breakfast and brunch.
  • Olympia Candy KitchenA historic downtown favorite for breakfast, lunch and homemade candy.
  • South Side Soda ShopA fun, old-fashioned diner and soda fountain.

Amish-country dining

  • Blue Gate Restaurant & BakeryIn Shipshewana. A great place to experience classic Amish-country cooking and homemade bakery treats.
  • Das Dutchman EssenhausIn Middlebury. Family-style Amish cooking, homemade noodles, chicken and delicious pies.

Especially recommended for out-of-town family

If you’re visiting Goshen for the first time, we especially recommend:

  1. Venturi
  2. Blue Gate Restaurant & Bakery
  3. Das Dutchman Essenhaus

Check Google for current hours, menus, reviews and directions before visiting, as hours can change.
