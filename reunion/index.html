Rounding Up the Leichty Clan!

It’s been too long since our whole extended family gathered in one place. We are bringing together all seven generations from Grandpa John and Grandma Salome’s legacy for an afternoon of reconnecting, sharing memories, and catching up.

Schedule of events

The day, minute by minute.

A relaxed afternoon with plenty of time to catch up — June 12, 2027, from 11:30 am – 4:00 pm.

  1. 11:30 AM

    Doors Open & Arrival

    Check in at the welcome table, grab your nametag, and start catching up with cousins and kin.

  2. 12:00 PM

    Family Photo

    Please be in the main pavilion hall promptly so we can capture our all-family picture with every branch and generation together.

  3. 12:15 PM

    Catered Lunch

    Sit down and enjoy a catered lunch together — no need to worry about bringing main dishes or heating food!

  4. 1:00 PM

    Family Storytelling

    A special hour to share favorite family stories, honor our elders, and revisit classic memories.

  5. 2:00 PM

    Family Connection & Games

    An open afternoon featuring yard games including cornhole and giant Jenga, board games, trivia, and time to visit our family memory tables and photo albums.

  6. 3:45 PM

    Prizes & Wrap-Up

    Celebrating our game champions, special recognitions, and closing announcements.

  7. 4:00 PM

    Family Farewells

    Hugs, goodbyes, and safe travels. Our designated cleanup crew will handle pack-down afterward, so everyone can relax and visit right up until departure.

What to bring

Just three things.

We’re taking care of lunch, so all you need to bring is what matters most.

Family Memorabilia

Old photos, albums, scrapbooks, or heirlooms to display on the shared Memory Table.

Comfortable Attire

We will have both indoor pavilion seating and lawn space for games outside.

Your Favorite Memories

Think of a story or favorite memory of Grandpa John, Grandma Salome, or growing up in the family to share during our storytelling hour.

Questions?

We’re here to help.
