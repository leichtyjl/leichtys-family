Travel & lodging

Planning your travel.

Practical info to help family coming in from out of town plan their trip.

Lodging

Everyone is welcome to make their own lodging arrangements for the weekend of our family reunion in Goshen, Indiana, on Saturday, June 12, 2027. Here are a few options to consider (each opens in Google Maps):

  • Hampton Inn Goshen3.6 miles · 1968 Lincolnway E · 574-533-8800Comfortable hotel with breakfast, indoor pool, and fitness center.
  • Holiday Inn Express Hotel & Suites2.1 miles · 1415 Lincolnway E · 574-535-9200Family-friendly option with breakfast, indoor pool, and suites.
  • Fairfield Inn & Suites by Marriott2 miles · 2110 Keystone Dr. · 574-538-8800A convenient hotel near the park with comfortable rooms.
  • The Inn on South 5th1.3 miles · 320 S 5th St · 574-536-5998Charming historic bed & breakfast in walking distance of downtown.

You can also check Airbnb for vacation rentals or use Google to search for additional hotels and lodging options in the Goshen area. Please choose whichever option works best for your family — these are suggestions only, with no reserved room blocks or special rates.

Travel by air

South Bend International Airport (SBN) — The closest airport with regular commercial flights, approximately 30–40 miles from Goshen. This is probably the easiest airport for most family members flying in.

Fort Wayne International Airport (FWA) — The second-best airport option after South Bend International Airport.

Other options to check include:

  • Kalamazoo/Battle Creek International Airport (AZO)
  • Gerald R. Ford International Airport in Grand Rapids (GRR)
  • Chicago Midway International Airport (MDW)
  • Chicago O’Hare International Airport (ORD)

Check flight prices and schedules for SBN first, followed by FWA. It may also be worth comparing the other airports for better fares or nonstop flights.
